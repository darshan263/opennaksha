    Town Planning & Estate

    1. Home
    2. Town Planning & Estate

    The Town Planning Department of Navsari Municipal Corporation (NMC) is responsible for guiding the planned growth and development of the city. It plays a vital role in shaping Navsari's future by managing land use, regulating construction, and ensuring sustainable urban development and Green space in accordance with state and national planning guidelines.

    The department aims to create a balanced, well-connected, and inclusive city by implementing long-term development plans and promoting efficient use of land and infrastructure.

    Core Responsibilities

    • Preparation & Implementation of Development Plans

      Long-term master Development plans and layout designs for urban expansion

    • Town Planning (TP) Schemes

      Planning and execution of TP schemes in newly developing areas

    • Building Plan & Building Use Permissions 

      Scrutiny and approval of residential, commercial, and industrial construction plans as per GDCR

    • Zoning & Land Use Management

      Allocation of land for housing, public utilities, green zones, and commercial use

    • Regularization & Encroachment Control

      Resolving unauthorized construction issues and ensuring proper utilization of urban land

    • Public Amenities & Infrastructure Planning

      Co-ordinating roads, drainage, open spaces, parks, and other essential urban services

    Department Vision

    To develop Navsari as a modern, efficient, and inclusive urban center with well-planned infrastructure, safe housing, and accessible civic amenities for all citizens.

    Services Offered:

    Development permission & land use certificate

    • Approval of building plans
    • Regularization of existing Building structures
    • Information on current and proposed DP & TP schemes
    • Grievance redressal for planning-related issues

TP Scheme

Town Planning Evolution Timeline

  • 1915
    Bombay Town Planning Act 1915

    Bombay Town Planning Act, 1915 enacted by Presidency of Bombay had a piecemeal approach of planning and only had a concept of town planning scheme incorporated.

  • 1920
    1st Town Planning Scheme

    First Town Planning Scheme prepared for Jamalpur.

  • 1954
    Bombay Town Planning Act 1954

    Since Bombay TP Act 1915 provided for growth of part of city only, it was repealed by Bombay TP Act 1954. The new act made it obligatory to prepare a Development Plan for the entire city.

    It led to the first attempts at town planning in Ahmedabad and provided for 20 town planning schemes and several other Development Plans.

  • 1965
    First Development Plan by AMC

    The first Development Plan for AMC was formulated and later revised in 1983.

  • 1976
    Gujarat Town Planning and Urban Development Act, 1976

    After Gujarat became a state in 1960, this act led to the formation of area/urban development authorities. 138 town planning schemes were devised for the newly constituted Ahmedabad Urban Development Authority (AUDA).

  • 1987
    Formation of AUDA

    Ahmedabad Urban Development Authority (AUDA) was officially formed, and the first Development Plan 2011 was enacted under AUDA.

  • 2002
    First Revised Development Plan 2011

    The first Development Plan 2011 was revised by AUDA.

  • 2014
    Second Revised Development Plan 2021

    The second revised Development Plan 2021 was sanctioned by the Government of Gujarat.

History of Town Planning Scheme & Development Plan

Earlier Scenario

  • Chaotic Growth.
  • Inadequate Resources for Infrastructure.
  • Inadequate Land for Roads.
  • Insufficient Data for Planning.

Implementation Initiatives Process

To enable rapid implementation of TP Schemes, AUDA initiated reforms in the Town Planning Act. These reforms were supported by Govt. of Gujarat

  • It makes possible to take possession of land for roads and for plots reserved for social infrastructure such as school, dispensary, fire brigade, public utility place as earmarked in the Draft Town Planning Scheme soon as a Draft TP Scheme is sanctioned.
  • AUDA Can recover cost of Infrastructure provisions in the form of land. This method of cost recovery is now widely accepted by land owners/citizens.

Preparation and Implementation of TP Schemes with usage of new advanced technologies.

  • Total Station survey is conducted right at start for upcoming TP Schemes.
  • GIS based preparation of TPS and Development.

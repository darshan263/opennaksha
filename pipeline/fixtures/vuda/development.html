Development Plan of Vuda

1st Development Plan

  • Vadodara Urban Development Authority is constituted under the provisions of the Gujarat town Planning and Urban Development act 1976.

  • Started functioning from 1st February 1978.

  • The draft Development plan was prepared and published on 17-5-79.

  • Approved by the Government on 22-12-83 and in force from 25-11-84

  • The Preparation of 1st Development Plan was based on Coming twenty years duration i.e. projected population up to year 2000 and their necessities were taken care.

1st Revised Development Plan 2011
  • To Asses and Visualize the progress of the development Plan proposals, the review of the implementation of the development Plan proposals periodically shall be made by the Authority.
  • As per the provision of the Gujarat town Planning and Urban Development act Under section–21, once in ten years Development plan is to be revised.
  • VUDA has prepared development plan based on latest information regarding existing Roads, land use pattern, Population etc, and adequate care taking to ensure public participation from Various Sections of the Society including Experts, local bodies, Government and Semi Government Organization.
  • The objections and suggestions received were processed by the Authority keeping in mind the over frame work of the Development plan proposals.
  • Final Draft Revised Development plan Submitted to the Government on 22-12-94.
  • which was Sectioned by Government with notifications No. GH/V/171 of 1996/DVP/1294/4036/L dated 25-10-96 is in force since 26-11-96.

  2nd Revised Draft Development Plan – 2031

  

  • As per the provision of the Gujarat town planning and Urban development act under section-21, once in ten years, development plan is to be revised VUDA has decided and started to prepare IInd revised development plan.

  • Published in Govt. Gazette under  Section -13 of the act on dt. 24-11-2006 and received objections and suggestions from public.

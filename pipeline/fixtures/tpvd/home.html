Workshop Organized on CORS Network by Survey of India

October 16, 2025

A workshop was organized at the Town Planning and Valuation Department in collaboration with the Survey of India on the Continuously Operating Reference Station (CORS) Network. The session focused on GNSS-based high-accuracy positioning, RTK services, and applications of CORS in land surveying and town planning. Experts explained the benefits of CORS in improving survey accuracy, efficiency, and integration with GIS-based planning systems. The workshop enhanced technical awareness among officers for adopting modern geospatial technologies in departmental activities.

Town Planning Schemes (TPS)

Town Planning Schemes (TPS) is a micro-level planning tool, used to implement the proposals of Development Plan. The planning proposal of the broader level is proposed in Development Plan. This proposal given broadly areas under different use zones, reservations for different purposes and road, infrastructure network. It is widely acknowledged model for urban development and implementing the proposals from the Development Plan. 

The Basic Concept of
Town Planning Scheme is pooling together all the land under different ownerships and redistributing it in a properly reconstituted form, after deducting the land required for open spaces, social infrastructures, services, housing for the socially and economically weaker sections, and road network. This entire process of preparation of TPS and finalization is driven by principle of natural justice, which enables the appropriate authority to develop land through readjustment and with public participation, without fully acquiring it and gives it a positive control over the design and the timing of the urban growth.

Preparation of TPS

For preparation of TPS, land parcels with common ownership are marked with survey number / original plot number on a map. In the layout plan, taking out the area for roads and streets and public and semi-public spaces, the remaining area is planned in regular plots known as final plots. The final plots though reduced in size, better in shape, enhanced buildability and accessibility are allocated to the land owners, preferably in close proximity to their original plots. The owner also gets compensation for the area reduced for public spaces and roads. Since the reconstituted plot has the better accessibility and good potential for development, its monetary value gets enhanced. The difference between enhanced value and the original value after reconstitution i.e. semi-final value is termed increment. Part of such increment in land value is contributed for the cost of development work in the scheme. Under the GTPUD Act, it is provided that the landowners will receive/contribute the net amount of the increment value of the plot worked out after deducting the amount of compensation payable for the loss in area. Therefore, TPS is also conceptualized as a joint venture between the appropriate authority and the owners of land, who voluntarily agree to pool their land, redistribute the reconstituted plots of land among themselves and share the development cost.

Key Components of a TPS

Affordable Housing and Social Inclusion

Each TPs earmarks land parcels for housing of socially and economically weaker sections, thereby promoting social inclusion

Infrastructure Planning

Outlines required infrastructure like roads, water supply, sewage systems, and public utilities to support development.

Transportation Planning

It addresses the movement of people and goods by earmarking appropriate Rights of Way (ROWs)

Environmental Considerations

Incorporates measures to protect natural resources, manage environmental impacts, and promote sustainability.

The TPS Process

Identification and Delineation of Area for TP Scheme

Town Planning (TP) Schemes are delineated based on their purpose—such as

greenfield expansion, infill development, infrastructure, social amenities, special projects, or post-disaster recovery—shaping the size, boundary, and land use.

Existing Situation Analysis

Analysis for a Draft TP Scheme involves detailed surveys and mapping of land use, buildings, ownership, topography, environment, infrastructure, transport, and informal activities. It helps identify suitable and unsuitable areas for development, assess existing amenities, and plan networks, utilities, and social needs. Jantri and transaction rate analysis further supports land allocation and value optimization.

DECLARATION OF INTENTION

this include purpose, delineation, surveying and preparation of index map and consultation with chief town planner, declaration of intention,

Planning, Design and Land Reconstitution

this is about how to integrate street network design, locating physical infrastructure, social amenities, parks and open spaces, Informal activities, EWS housing etc while preparing a comprehensive T P Scheme layout . it also include deriving land deductions/contributions and carrying out land reconstitution

Valuation, Cost and Finance for TP Scheme Implementation

Principles of valuation, reconstitution of land, financial management and calculating the incremental contribution by the plot owners are key aspects of this stage.

Owners meeting, Publication, Modification, and Submission of Draft T P Scheme

Consultation with land owners and beneficiaries, Receiving suggestions and objections, carrying out required modifications and submission of the Draft T P Scheme

Sanctioning of Draft TP Scheme and Appointment of TPO

The Government reviews and sanctions the Draft TP Scheme and appoints a Town Planning Officer.

Preparation of Preliminary TP Scheme

The TPO invites objections, conducts hearings, records decisions, and publishes the revised Draft TP Scheme for public inspection.

Preparation of Final TP Scheme

The TPO prepares the Final TP Scheme, finalizing compensation and contribution for all plots based on value changes and benefits

First TP Scheme of India

In Gujarat, Town Planning Schemes as an instrument for urban development has a long history. The first Town Planning Scheme was taken up as early as in 1917 for Jamalpur area of Ahmedabad city. Perhaps Jamalpur area Town Planning Scheme was also the first TPS in the country. The Bombay Town Planning Act, 1915 provided for growth and development of various parts of the city which facilitated taking up Town Planning Schemes. The Act was modified and re-enacted in 1954 which made it obligatory for each local authority to carry out a survey of the area within its jurisdiction for preparation of development plan.
